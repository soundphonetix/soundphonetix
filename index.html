I've engineered for Grammy-winning artists, produced and released full projects from the ground up, and built a workflow that delivers professional results in any environment — from purpose-built studios to unfamiliar venues on the road.

As founder of Soundphonetix LLC, I bring both the technical rigor and the entrepreneurial drive to every project I take on.
